DIAGNOSIS / 03

Inspiration is the polite word.

The nice words were made for people who sleep. Inspiration. Passion. Talent. Cute. Put them on a mug.

The rest of us don’t get inspired. We get infected. A song’s bridge cracks your ribs. A stranger’s laugh becomes a protagonist. A flickering streetlight drafts you into a noir film.

You don’t have a hobby. You have a hostage situation.

This book maps that condition.

FRAMEWORK / 04

THE ECHO CYCLE

The book follows a four-part loop that shows how creative compulsion moves through the body, the work, and the world.

⌁ BREACH

Something lands, uninvited, and won’t let go.

⇌ GLITCH

You try to copy it. Your hands betray you.

↻ DRIFT

You stop fixing the flaw and start feeding it. That’s voice.

⇒ UNLEASH

You throw it like a match and let it go.
